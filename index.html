Good to know

What's included?

Wi-Fi, pool access and housekeeping. Ask us about electricity for monthly stays.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates. We confirm availability, then take a deposit by bank transfer or card link to hold the room.

Peak season?

In December, July and August nightly rates are higher and monthly stays are +20%.

Minimum stay?

2 nights. Prices shown are "from" rates and can be higher on weekends, holidays and in peak season. Send your dates for an exact price.

Weekly and monthly rates?

Stay 7 nights or more and we offer the weekly rate; 30 nights or more, the monthly rate. Both are shown on each room above.
