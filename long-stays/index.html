Good to know

How much is a monthly stay?

From IDR 20.000.000 a month for a standard suite, up to IDR 55.000.000 for the two-bedroom Penthouse with a private rooftop jacuzzi. In December, July and August monthly stays are +20%.

What is included?

Furnished suite, Wi-Fi, pool access and housekeeping. Ask us about electricity for monthly stays.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates. We confirm availability and hold the room with a 30% deposit by bank transfer or card link.

Do you have weekly rates?

Yes: about 10% less than booking 7 single nights. Prices for every room are in the table above.

Do returning guests get a discount?

Yes, returning guests get 10% off when they book direct with us.

Where is Onyx?

On Jalan Padang Linjong in Canggu, 2.3 km (about 6 minutes) from Echo Beach, Batu Bolong and Canggu Beach, with cafés, restaurants, yoga and spas on the street.
